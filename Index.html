FOOD SAFETY INNOVATION

A Smarter Approach to Food Safety Starts Here.

OmegaTech is developing a technology concept designed to help identify potentially harmful substances in food and explore new approaches to accessible food-safety screening.

Development Stage: Concept

Be in control of what's on your plate. Feel safer about what's in your food.

Why food safety matters

Households, food-service businesses and food producers all depend on food being safe. Appearance, smell and taste alone cannot reliably reveal certain contaminants. Different hazards need different testing methods, so a single handheld detector for many substances would require substantial research and validation. OmegaTech exists to explore whether that gap can be narrowed responsibly.

Mission

To advance the development of intelligent food-safety technology that could help people and organisations make better-informed decisions about the safety of food.

Vision

To contribute to a future in which scientifically validated food-safety screening technologies become more accessible to people and organisations around the world.

Commitment

Feasibility before scaling, honest limits, and validation against accepted reference methods.

OmegaTech is currently at the concept stage and is seeking development funding.

The technology concept

The concept is a portable, user-friendly device, possibly with a touchscreen, intended to help screen food for certain potentially harmful substances. Target contaminants, detectable concentrations, sample preparation, detection limits and measurement methods have not yet been established. Actual capabilities will depend on research and validation.

Conceptual workflow (final method depends on feasibility research)

1. Prepare

An appropriate sample or measurement setup, depending on the eventual design.

2. Analyse

A scientifically validated sensing and analysis process.

3. Interpret

An interpretable result with clearly defined limitations.

Development directions

Intelligent Sensing Research

Analytical, spectroscopic or chemical sensing methods, where scientifically appropriate.

AI-Assisted Data Analysis

Signal interpretation, with attention to data quality and uncertainty. AI is used only where its role can be justified.

Accessible Screening

Exploring whether screening can become simpler for non-specialists.

Validation and Responsible Engineering

Calibration, repeatability, reliability and laboratory reference testing.

Detection is not neutralisation

Detecting a contaminant and safely neutralising it are separate research challenges. Neutralisation would need contaminant-specific methods, evidence of effectiveness, and proof that treatment creates no new hazards. OmegaTech makes no claim that the concept can neutralise harmful substances.

Scientific validation

Development will require reference methods, controlled testing, independent expertise, and evaluation of false positives, false negatives, sensitivity and specificity.
